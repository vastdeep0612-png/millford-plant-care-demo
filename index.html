Houseplants, visited weekly

Someone comes by, waters the plants, and leaves a short note.

Millford Plant Care is a fictional one-person service for homes and small offices. The visit covers watering, trimming, and repotting. This page is a demo, not a client website.

Ask about a visit See what is included

On a visit

  • Watering matched to the pot and the light
  • Yellow leaves and spent stems removed
  • A note of anything that needs a bigger pot

Services

Three visits. The address and the plant count are agreed before the day.

Home visit

One address. Up to 12 plants. About 45 minutes.

Office visit

One room. Up to 20 plants. Before the office opens, or after it closes.

Repotting

Pots you already own. Soil is included. Sick plants are not taken away.

How a first visit works

  1. You send the address, the plant count, and a photo of the room.
  2. The visit checks light, pots, and anything crawling on the leaves.
  3. You get a short written note of what was watered, trimmed, or left alone.

What this is not

Millford is not a real shop. No client commissioned this page. The name, the town, and the prices below are made up so the layout can be reviewed.
